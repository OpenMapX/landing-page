OpenMapX is an open-source mapping app built on OpenStreetMap and other open
datasets. Search for places, get directions, explore public transport, and look
around with street-level imagery. You can use the public map or host an instance
with the services and data you choose.

The project is experimental. Features, live information, and coverage depend on
the server's enabled integrations and the sources available for your area.
The [feature guide](/features) explains what the project supports and where its
limits are.

[Open the map](https://openmapx.com), read the
[self-hosting guide](https://docs.openmapx.org/install/getting-started/), or help
with an issue or contribution on [GitHub](https://github.com/OpenMapX/openmapx).
